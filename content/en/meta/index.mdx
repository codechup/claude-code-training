Meta is the part of the course about the course. It walks through this repository's own `.claude/` directory, plans, CI, and deployment as a worked example of everything taught in Level 4 — the same plan-file and `STATE.md` machinery you read about in `m18-multi-session` is what produced the page you are reading.

It also covers how to propose or fix a lesson and what the review pipeline checks before a change ships, and it keeps a single index of every source cited anywhere on the site, each with the date it was verified.

There is no prerequisite for Meta; it is written for anyone curious about the site's own construction, not only for people who have finished the curriculum.
