The Playbook is not a fifth level — it is the reference you come back to once you already know the material. Everything on these pages is aggregated from the lessons: nothing is taught here for the first time, and every entry links to the lesson it came from.

- **[Decision trees](/en/playbook/decision-trees/)** — which mechanism to reach for: `CLAUDE.md` vs. a rule vs. a skill vs. a hook vs. an agent vs. MCP (D091), plus the delegation and model/effort choices, as inline theme-aware diagrams you can search with `Ctrl+F`.
- **[Best-practice digest](/en/playbook/best-practices/)** — the strongest practices from each level, one line each, in the order you meet them.
- **[Anti-pattern catalogue](/en/playbook/anti-patterns/)** — the failure modes that recur across modules, each with its fix, its source, and its lesson.
- **[Changed since 2025](/en/playbook/changelog/)** — every superseded behaviour the course tracks, with what a stale tutorial gets wrong.
- **[Glossary](/en/playbook/glossary/)** — every English technical term the course keeps as-is, defined in one line.

Read the decision trees after Level 1, the best-practice digest at the end of each level, and the changelog whenever something you read elsewhere contradicts a lesson here. The Playbook is written to be skimmed more than read start to finish.
