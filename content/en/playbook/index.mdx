The Playbook is not a fifth level — it is the reference you come back to once you already know the material: which mechanism to reach for (`CLAUDE.md` vs. a rule vs. a skill vs. a hook vs. an agent vs. MCP), a digest of best practices for each pair of levels, and the full catalogue of anti-patterns with their fixes.

It also holds the two living pages that grow as the rest of the course is written: the glossary, which collects every English technical term used across the site with a one-line explanation, and the "Changed since 2025" page, which tracks behaviour that shifted after most tutorials on the web were written.

Read the Playbook after Level 1 for the decision trees, and again after Level 4 for the deeper checklists — it is written to be skimmed more than read start to finish.
