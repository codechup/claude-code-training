This module coordinates more than one agent on a single task. You will learn the Workflow tool's `agent`, `parallel`, and `pipeline` primitives, the `ultracode` setting that plans dynamic workflows for you, cross-session messaging between agent teams, and build a review-verify-fix pipeline before looking at how to design roles and hand-offs that do not fail silently.
