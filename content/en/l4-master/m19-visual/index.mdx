This module ships work you can look at rather than only read: publishing and updating an artifact and reading comments on it, generating a design canvas from a brief, driving a real site with Claude in Chrome and recording what happened, and the chart-and-dashboard discipline behind `/dataviz`.

1. `01-artifacts` — Publishing an artifact, updating it, comments, capabilities (20 min, core).
2. `02-design-canvas` — `/design`: a canvas from a brief, approval, tokens (20 min, core).
3. `03-chrome-automation` — Driving a site with Claude in Chrome; GIF recordings (15 min, core).
4. `04-dataviz` — `/dataviz` and chart discipline (10 min, advanced).
